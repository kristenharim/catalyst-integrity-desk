New here? What am I looking at — and how to read it

Every clinical-stage biotech thesis rests on one bet: we have enough cash to last until our trial finishes. The left half comes from SEC filings, the right half from the public trial registry, where the company sets the finish date and can change it whenever it likes. This desk recomputes both halves and shows when the bet has quietly broken. A healthy row is green and boring; the point is the red ones.

ContractOne company's bet that its cash lasts until its trial's filed finish date.
GapMonths of cash left minus months until the trial finishes. Negative (red) means the money runs out first; positive (green) means funded with room.
Runway bandHow many months of cash the company has left, as a range, read from its SEC filings.
Registered primary completionThe date the company filed with the public trial registry for when its trial finishes. Not the same as when results are announced.
RevisionsHow many times the company has changed that filed finish date.
BreachA bet that has broken: the date it was counting on has already slipped past.
LapsedA filed finish date that is already in the past and still shown as current.
FlaggedA number the desk does not trust enough to rank, shown anyway rather than hidden.
UnresolvedYou asked about a company, but no matching trial was found in the registry.
